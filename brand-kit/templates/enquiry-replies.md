# Reply templates

Three replies cover almost every enquiry. Keep them short: the site has already done the
explaining, so these exist to get to a conversation.

Brand voice rules apply. Plain sentences, no urgency, permission leads, and never the
phrase "AI-generated".

---

## 1. First reply to a full enquiry

Aim to send within one working day.

```
Subject: Re: your Lyrical enquiry

Hi NAME,

Thanks for getting in touch, and for the detail on CATALOGUE or SONG.

Before anything else: nothing gets made without your approval, and we only build a voice
model from a catalogue we have written permission to use. If that is straightforward on
your side, the rest is easy.

The usual first step is one song. You send the finished master and the language you want,
we come back with a version you can play against the original. From there you decide
whether it goes further.

Two questions so I can give you something specific:

1. Which song would you start with, and which language?
2. Who signs off on the likeness and recording rights, you or the artist directly?

Happy to jump on a call if that is easier.

NAME
Lyrical
```

---

## 2. Reply to an examples request

They gave you an email and the languages they care about, nothing else. Ask for the minimum.

```
Subject: Your Lyrical examples

Hi,

Thanks for asking. Sending examples across shortly.

One thing that would help me send something relevant rather than generic: what kind of
catalogue are you working with, and are you an artist, a manager, or at a label?

If you already have a song in mind, tell me which and I will point at the closest thing
we have.

NAME
Lyrical
```

---

## 3. Not right now

Worth sending properly. A clean no keeps the door open, and this category is small.

```
Subject: Re: your Lyrical enquiry

Hi NAME,

Thanks for the detail. Being straight with you: this is not something we would take on
well right now, because REASON.

I would rather say that than take it and do it badly. If SPECIFIC CHANGE happens, come
back to me and it becomes an easy yes.

NAME
Lyrical
```

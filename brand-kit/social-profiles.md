A song shouldn't stop at a border.

Lyrical recreates a finished record in another language so it sounds like the artist
genuinely recorded it that way. The melody, the rhythm and the phrasing are kept intact,

We start from catalogues that have already proven they connect. Nothing is made without

vocal stem for their own engineer, reference versions for A/B against the original, and a

mixing control. We hand over stems, not a locked file.

One song, or a whole catalogue.

## Instagram content pillars

Four repeatable formats, so posting does not require inventing something each time.

1. **Before and after.** The most persuasive thing the company owns. Blocked until there is
   cleared audio, and worth pushing hard to unblock.
2. **The craft.** How a lyric is rewritten to sit on an existing melody, syllable by
   syllable. Shows expertise without needing a finished product.
3. **The rights position.** Plain statements about permission and approval. This is the
   differentiator, and almost nobody in the category leads with it.
4. **Type-only quote cards.** Use the post template. Cheap to produce, and they keep the
   grid looking deliberate between the other three.

Never post anything implying an artist is involved when they are not. Everything shown must
be authorised, or the whole proposition falls over.

Email signatures

Three signatures, matching the site and the automated emails Lyrical already sends. Built for email rather than for the web: tables, inline styles, and no webfonts, because Fraunces and Archivo do not exist in a mail client and a signature that falls back badly looks worse than one that never tried.

The logo is a real image, hosted at lyricalglobal.com/brand/lyrical-lockup.png. That matters more than it sounds: the image is the actual wordmark set in Fraunces, whereas a text version has to fall back to Georgia, because no mail client will load a webfont. The image is the only way a signature shows your real typography.

The cost is that Outlook desktop blocks remote images by default for senders outside your organisation, so some recipients see the alt text until they click "Download pictures". Gmail, Apple Mail and most webmail load it without asking. A text-only fallback is at the bottom of this page if you would rather never risk it.

How to install in Zoho

  1. Zoho Mail → Settings (the gear) → Signatures.
  2. New Signature, and name it.
  3. Come back here, select the signature block below from just above the logo to just below the last line, and copy.
  4. Paste into the Zoho editor. It keeps the formatting.
  5. Set it to apply to new mail and to replies, then save.

If pasting ever loses the styling, open the "raw HTML" panel under each signature and paste the code into Zoho's source view instead.

What is deliberately not in here

No tagline, no phone number and no rights line, as you chose. A signature is read once and then skipped forever, so every line you add dilutes the ones that matter. Name, role, address and site is what a recipient needs in order to reply or to look you up.

No social icons either. They are separate images, they are the ones that most often break, and Lyrical has nothing to link to yet.

Colours are the locked brand tokens: indigo #4433D6 for links, graphite #1C1A19 for names, and a muted warm grey for roles. The logo is served from lyricalglobal.com, so the URL is ours and will not disappear.

Email signatures

Three signatures, matching the site and the automated emails Lyrical already sends. Built for email rather than for the web: tables, inline styles, and no webfonts, because Fraunces and Archivo do not exist in a mail client and a signature that falls back badly looks worse than one that never tried.

The mark is the character ≈, not an image. It always renders, needs nothing hosted, cannot become a grey placeholder box in a client that blocks images, and adds no attachment to every email you send. Your enquiry emails already do this, so all Lyrical mail matches.

How to install in Zoho

  1. Zoho Mail → Settings (the gear) → Signatures.
  2. New Signature, and name it.
  3. Come back here, select the signature block below from just above the ≈ to just below the last line, and copy.
  4. Paste into the Zoho editor. It keeps the formatting.
  5. Set it to apply to new mail and to replies, then save.

If pasting ever loses the styling, open the "raw HTML" panel under each signature and paste the code into Zoho's source view instead.

What is deliberately not in here

No tagline, no phone number and no rights line, as you chose. Worth knowing why that is the right call: a signature is read once and then skipped forever, so every line you add is a line that dilutes the ones that matter. Name, role, address, site is what a recipient actually needs in order to reply or to look you up.

No social icons either. They are images, they get blocked, and Lyrical has nothing to link to yet.

Colours are the locked brand tokens: indigo #4433D6 for the mark and links, graphite #1C1A19 for names, and a muted warm grey for roles. Georgia stands in for Fraunces and Helvetica for Archivo, which is exactly what your automated emails already do.

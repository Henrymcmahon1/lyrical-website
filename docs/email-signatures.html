Email signatures

Three signatures, matching the site and the automated emails Lyrical already sends.

These carry their own cream background on purpose. Without it the signature broke in dark mode: the wordmark is graphite and your name is graphite, so on a dark compose window both disappeared entirely, and indigo links on a dark ground fail contrast, which your own brand rules already forbid.

A signature cannot ask what colour the surrounding email is. So it brings its own ground, and every colour on it is then guaranteed. The grey checkerboard behind each block below is only there so you can see it is opaque; it is not part of the signature.

How to install in Zoho

  1. Zoho Mail → Settings → Signatures → New Signature.
  2. Come back here and select the signature itself: from just above the logo to just below the web address. The finished-looking block, not the code.
  3. Paste into the Zoho editor. You should see the logo. If you see a wall of code, you copied from a “Raw HTML” panel by mistake.
  4. Fill in Associated From addresses for both new mail and replies, or the signature attaches to nothing.
  5. Save.

What is deliberately not in here

No tagline, no phone number and no rights line, as you chose. A signature is read once and skipped forever, so every line you add dilutes the ones that matter.

No social icons. They are more images to block, and Lyrical has nothing to link to yet.

Locked brand tokens throughout: cream #F7EFE1 ground, graphite #1C1A19 names, indigo #4433D6 links, and a muted warm grey for roles. The logo is served from lyricalglobal.com, so the URL is ours and will not move.
